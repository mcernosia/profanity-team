Write copy for Profanity's owned channels in the studio voice:

**Voice rules:**
- Direct, confident, slightly irreverent — never corporate
- Position the studio upstream: creative thinking partner, not production vendor
- Every sentence does work — no throat-clearing, no "at Profanity, we believe..."

- "Passionate about..."

- Jargon that clients don't use
- Em-dashes (—): banned. Use a comma, a period, or rewrite.
