# Pricing Framework — Profanity Creative

## Fundamentals

- **Minimum engagement (direct-to-client):** $15,000
- **Freelance / contractor day rate:** **$900/day** — used for white-label or sub-contract engagements (e.g., Territory Studio). Distinct from project-based direct-to-client work, which uses the 3-tier model below.
- **Standard project duration:** 3–4 weeks
- **Pricing model:** Always present a 3-tier structure. The goal is to land the middle option.
- **Never lock in a number without confirming with Mark.** Proposals use ranges or tiers; a line in the proposal always notes "final investment confirmed upon project kickoff."

## Scope-creep response (verbatim)

When a client asks for something outside the agreed scope:

> "Happy to explore that. It's a bit outside the original scope, so I can price that as an add-on or we can swap it against one of the existing deliverables."

The "swap against existing deliverables" option is real and frequently the right move. Don't drop it.

## 3-Tier Pricing Structure

Always name the tiers clearly so the client understands the value difference, not just the price difference. Suggested naming:

| Tier | Label | What it includes |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Tier 1 (Low) | Essentials | Core deliverable, limited revisions, standard timeline |
| Tier 2 (Middle — Target) | Full Production | Full scope, standard revisions, some add-ons included |
| Tier 3 (High) | Premium / Expanded | Full scope + extras (additional formats, extended revisions, expedited timeline, raw files, etc.) |

**Pricing spread guideline:**
- Tier 1 should be the floor — minimum viable scope at minimum margin. Start at $15k for direct-to-client.
- Tier 2 should be your real number — priced for the full job with healthy margin.
- Tier 3 should be aspirational — what would make this project feel easy and well-resourced.
- Typical spread: Tier 1 at ~65-70% of Tier 2, Tier 3 at ~130-140% of Tier 2.

**Example spread (mid-complexity product film):**
- Tier 1: $17,500
- Tier 2: $25,000 ← target
- Tier 3: $34,000

## Phase-Based Hour Estimates

Use these as a starting guide. Complexity flags (see scope-flags.md) add time.

| Phase | Description | Est. Hours (Simple) | Est. Hours (Complex) |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Discovery & Concept | Brief review, concepting, moodboards | 8–12 | 16–24 |
| Pre-production | Storyboard, animatic, asset planning | 12–20 | 24–40 |
| Production | Modeling, texturing, lighting, animation | 40–60 | 80–120+ |
| Revision Rounds | Typically 2–3 rounds | 8–16 | 16–30 |
| Delivery & Finishing | Rendering, compositing, file prep, export | 8–12 | 16–24 |

---

## Revision Policy (Standard)

- **Concept/pre-production:** 2 rounds included
- **Production:** 2 rounds included
- **Additional revisions:** Quoted separately, typically $150–$250/hour depending on scope
- Always define in proposal what a "revision round" means (consolidated notes, not rolling changes)

---

## Add-Ons to Flag in Proposals

These are common upsells that should appear as optional line items:

- Additional color/finish variations (+$500–$2,500 per variation depending on complexity)
- Additional formats/aspect ratios (e.g., vertical cut for social) (+$500–$1,500)
- Raw/source file delivery (+$1,000–$3,000)
- Extended revision rounds (+$150–$250/hr)
- Rush fee (under 2 weeks): +20–30% of total
- Licensed music / sound design (variable — flag as TBD)
- VO/script (if needed): flag as add-on

---

## Agency vs. Direct Client

- **Direct-to-client:** Full rates, $15k floor, Mark owns client relationship
- **Agency/production company:** May involve a markup layer; be clear about deliverables and who owns the client relationship
- **Repeat client:** Small goodwill discount is fine (5–10%), but don't erode the base rate

---

## How to Present Pricing in a Proposal

1. Lead with tiers clearly laid out — label them, don't just list three numbers
2. Describe what's in each tier briefly (2–3 bullet points max)
3. Add a note: *"Final investment is confirmed at project kickoff once scope is aligned."*
4. List optional add-ons below the tiers as a separate section
5. Payment terms (standard): 50% deposit to begin, 50% upon delivery

---

## Payment Terms (Standard)

- **50% deposit** due before work begins
- **50% balance** due upon final delivery
- Larger projects (>$30k) can offer a 3-payment structure: 40% / 40% / 20%
- Late payment: flag politely at 14 days, firmly at 30 days
