## 2D / character animation, title sequences

## Rights, buyouts, source files
- 2026-07-17: Full-rights buyout typically 200–300% of base project cost. Source files that work as a reusable template are often priced at up to 3x base. Copyright assignment > exclusive perpetual license. (shirabentley.com; businessofanimation.com; contra.com)

## Agency resale
- 2026-10-14: Agencies bill clients at least 2x freelancer cost (rule of thumb; 2015 article updated Jan 2026, stale origin). (https://sakasandcompany.com/profit-margin-on-freelancers)
