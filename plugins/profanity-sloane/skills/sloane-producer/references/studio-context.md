# Studio Context — Profanity Creative

## Who We Are

Profanity Creative is a lean 3D product visualization and motion studio based in Vermont, run by Mark Cernosia. The studio specializes in making products look incredible through high-end 3D rendering, animation, and visual storytelling. We work directly with brands, startups, and agencies — and we punch above our weight class.

**Positioning:** a lean studio with deep specialist craft — Mark Cernosia delivers the work directly, with a curated network of specialists pulled in when a project calls for it. Price the work for what it delivers, not the headcount producing it.

The studio runs intentionally small. Mark leads creative direction and client relationships. Outside specialists (animators, riggers, sound designers, compositors) are brought in project-by-project. This keeps the work quality high and overhead low — but it also means every project needs to be priced to cover that model.

## What We Make

### Core Deliverables

| Deliverable | Description | Common Formats |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Product Visualization | Hero shots, beauty renders, lifestyle stills | PNG/TIFF, up to 8K, various aspect ratios |
| Product Animation | Feature films, 360° spins, exploder views | MP4/MOV, H.264 or ProRes, 24/25/30fps, typically 1080p or 4K |
| Brand Films | Short narrative films featuring the product | Same as above; sometimes with licensed music/VO |
| Explainer / Exploded Views | Technical breakdowns, assembly sequences | MP4/MOV |
| Social / Cut-downs | Recut versions for IG, TikTok, etc. | Vertical (9:16), Square (1:1), H.264 |
| Packaging Visualization | Product in-context or clean | PNG/TIFF or animated |

### Standard Technical Specs (Confirm Every Time)

- **Resolution:** 1080p (standard), 4K (premium/hero), 8K stills
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps (cinematic), 30fps (web/social), 60fps (if specified)
- **Color Space:** sRGB for digital, confirm if print/broadcast
- **Delivery Format:** MP4 (H.264) for web/social, ProRes 422 for agency/broadcast handoff
- **Still Format:** PNG-24 (transparency if needed), TIFF for print
- **Always confirm:** aspect ratio, looping (for social), audio requirements

**Rule:** Never assume delivery specs. Always ask or confirm in the brief.

## Typical Project Phases

1. **Discovery & Brief Alignment** — Get aligned on what the client actually wants (vs. what they say they want)
2. **Concept Development** — Visual direction, moodboards, style frames
3. **Pre-Production** — Storyboard, animatic, asset list, timeline confirmation
4. **Production** — Modeling, texturing, lighting, rigging, animation
5. **Revision Rounds** — Consolidated feedback, two rounds standard
6. **Delivery & Finishing** — Final renders, compositing, format prep, file delivery

## Specialist Roles (When to Bring Them In)

| Role | When needed |
|------|-------------|
| Animator | Any motion work beyond simple turntables |
| Rigger | Complex mechanical products, character-adjacent motion |
| Compositor | Multi-element shots, live-action integration |
| Sound Designer | Any film with audio (even subtle ambience) |
| VO/Talent | Explainer videos, narrative films |
| Copywriter | Script, on-screen text, any written narrative |

Specialist costs should be factored into the project price, not listed as pass-throughs unless the client specifically asks.

## Client Types

| Type | Notes |

## Mark's Voice — Reference Examples

**In a proposal:**
> "We're pumped about this one. The brief is right in our wheelhouse — this is the kind of work we built the studio to do."

**In a scope-creep email:**
> "Hey [Name] — just want to flag something before we get too far in. The new additions you mentioned are outside what we scoped originally, which means we'd need to talk about adjusting the budget. Happy to make it work, just want to be upfront about it."

**In a follow-up:**
> "Just checking in — wanted to make sure the latest round landed well on your end. Shoot me a note when you get a chance and we'll lock in next steps."

**General tone markers:**
- Contractions always: "we're," "it's," "let's," not "we are," "it is," "let us"
- Short sentences. No rambling.
- Warm opener, clear body, single CTA at the end
- Never "per my last email," never "as previously discussed," never "please advise"
- Occasional light personality ("love this project," "should be a fun one") — but never forced
