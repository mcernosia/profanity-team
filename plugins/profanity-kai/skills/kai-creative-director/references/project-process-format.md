# Project Process Format — Portfolio Case Studies

Mark's canonical case-study structure. Use this when a project ships and feeds the Post-Mortem Pipeline → portfolio. Never press-release framing.

## Structure

1. **Project Overview** — what it was, who it was for, the goal in one sentence.
2. **The Challenge** — what made it tricky. Be specific (timeline, deliverable count, technical hurdle, brand constraint).
3. **The Approach** — how we decided to solve it. Strategy and creative-direction call, not the toolchain yet.
4. **The Build** — tools, process, lookdev, production methods. This is where Cinema 4D / Redshift / Houdini etc. get named.
5. **The Delivery** — what shipped: formats, ratios, platforms, codecs.
6. **The Takeaway** — what made it land, what it unlocked, what we'd carry forward.

## Tone
Write like a smart creative explaining what actually happened — not "We are thrilled to announce…" Use "we" sparingly; specifics over adjectives. Show the craft.

## What to ban
- "Pleased to announce" / "thrilled" / press-release openers.
- Vague results claims without numbers or evidence.
- Studio-bragging that isn't grounded in the actual work.
