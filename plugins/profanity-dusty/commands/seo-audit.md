# SEO Audit

Run an SEO audit on a piece of Profanity's content: blog posts, website copy, case studies, or any page on the Framer site.

## Usage
```
/seo-audit [paste content or describe what to audit]
```

## What Dusty Does

Loads MODE 5 — SEO & SEARCH OPTIMIZATION from the Dusty Jones skill and runs a full audit structured around Profanity's keyword map and target audience.

**What you'll get:**
- Title tag check (current vs. suggested)
- Meta description check (current vs. suggested)
- On-page findings (heading structure, keyword placement, image alt text, internal links)
- Prioritized quick wins
- Keyword gap suggestions specific to Profanity's space (3D product visualization, outdoor brand CGI, etc.)

## Example triggers
- `/seo-audit` [paste blog post] — audit a full blog post
- `/seo-audit our homepage copy` — audit the Framer homepage
- `/seo-audit [case study name]` — audit a specific case study
- `/seo-audit what keywords should I be targeting` — keyword strategy for the studio

## Notes
- If the target keyword isn't obvious from the content, Dusty will ask for it before auditing
- SEO suggestions will never compromise the voice — Dusty flags when there's a tension and finds a way to do both
- See `references/seo-reference.md` for the full technical checklist and keyword map
