# Series: Frames From The Cutting Room Floor

A recurring side series of rejected, unused or "almost" frames. The hook is the honest reason the frame got cut. One post a week at most. It's a side series, not a content engine.

Mark's master template lives in the workspace at `Frames_From_The_Cutting_Room_Floor_Caption_Template.md`. If you can read the workspace, read that file first; it wins over this summary.

## Instagram

```
Frame from the cutting room floor. [WHAT IT IS / WHERE IN THE PROCESS]

[WHY IT GOT CUT. One honest, specific sentence.]

[THE DETAIL. One thing about the fix, the problem, or what it taught you.]

[WHAT IT BECAME. Final is on the grid / in the carousel / coming soon.]

[5-7 hashtags]
```

- Line 1 never changes. It's the series signature.
- "Why it got cut" is a real, specific reason. "Wasn't quite right" fails. "The stitching read as plastic at 2K" passes.
- Max 5 short lines. No product claims. The only CTA is pointing at the final.
- Hashtag pool (pick 5-7): #cgi #3dvisualization #productvisualization #cgiartist #behindthescenes #outtakes #cuttingroomfloor #octanerender #redshift
- Leave the studio name out of the caption so the series works on any account, CFC included.

## LinkedIn

Same opening line. Add one sentence on the decision and why a brand would care: this is the kind of call that never shows up in a final render but decides whether the product looks real. 3-5 hashtags from: #3dvisualization #productvisualization #cgi #productlaunch #creativeprocess

## Format

- Carousel: cut frame first, final last, so the swipe is the payoff. Single image is fine when there's no final yet.
- Reel option: 5-8 seconds flashing through the rejected frames, ending on the keeper. Use lines 1 and 2 of the caption.

## Where frames come from: the Social BTS Archive

The workspace holds `Social_BTS_Archive/`, built 2026-08-10 from Mark's screenshot folder. Read its `README_ARCHIVE_INDEX.md` before suggesting any frame.

**Hard rules:**

1. Only suggest files from the year folders (`2017`–`2026`). Those were reviewed and cleared.
2. Never suggest, open, describe or caption anything in `_REVIEW_QUARANTINE/`. It holds confidential client work, credentials and personal material. If Mark asks for something from there, tell him he has to clear the file himself first.
3. **Association risk:** some cleared frames show the same product as quarantined, branded ones (Carnival hull, Utah Jazz court, razr clay renders). Don't group frames from the same project into one carousel, or between consecutive posts, when that set would identify the client.
4. **Unreleased products:** a frame from a client project only goes up once the product launch is public or the client has cleared it. When in doubt, flag it and don't draft.
5. Only tag a brand after the final is published or they've cleared it.
6. Never suggest anything from Camp Mograph. It's paused.

When suggesting frames, give the exact file path, say why it fits the series, and give the likely "why it got cut" angle as a question Mark answers. You don't know why a frame was cut, so never make up the reason. Ask him.
