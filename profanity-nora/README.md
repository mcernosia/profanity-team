# profanity-nora

Nora Beckett — Social Media Manager for Profanity Creative.

Nora handles Instagram and LinkedIn for Profanity: a lean 3D product visualization studio run by Mark Cernosia out of Vermont. She writes in Mark's voice — direct, confident, slightly irreverent, never corporate — and focuses on inbound leads and industry credibility over follower count.

---

## Commands

| Command | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| `/post [project details]` | Write ready-to-publish captions for Instagram and LinkedIn |
| `/plan [optional context]` | Build a two-week content calendar with a content bank |
| `/launch [project details]` | Create a multi-post launch sequence for completed work |
| `/grow [question or target]` | Get no-BS growth and visibility advice |

---

## Usage

### `/post`
Give Nora details about a project — what it was, who it was for, what makes it interesting — and she'll write Instagram and LinkedIn captions you can post as-is, plus notes on timing and strategy.

```
/post Just finished a hydration pack render for a Colorado-based trail running brand.
Shot on a wet granite surface, heavy condensation detail.
```

### `/plan`
Tell her what's on the bench right now (or don't — she'll ask). She'll build a practical two-week schedule with specific post ideas, a content bank, and seasonal hooks worth tapping.

```
/plan We have the trail running project to launch, plus some WIP shots from a
new boot render we're halfway through.
```

### `/launch`
Give her the project name, client, and what assets you have available. She'll map out a posting sequence with caption drafts for each post — teaser, reveal, BTS, detail shots — plus reel and outreach suggestions.

```
/launch YETI collab just wrapped — hero shots, a BTS lighting test video,
and detail crops. Client is okay to tag.
```

### `/grow`
Ask anything about platform strategy, reaching specific brands, or what's actually worth Mark's time.

```
/grow Should I be posting on TikTok?
/grow How do I get in front of the marketing team at Patagonia?
/grow What content actually drives inquiries vs. just likes?
```

---

## Platforms

- **Instagram** — Primary. Where the work lives.
- **LinkedIn** — Where the business case gets made.

Nora does not recommend TikTok, X/Twitter, or heavy investment in other platforms for Profanity's current goals. She'll tell you why if you ask.

## Setup

No environment variables or external services required. This plugin works out of the box.
