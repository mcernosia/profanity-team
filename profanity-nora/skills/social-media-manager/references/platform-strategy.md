# Platform Strategy Reference

## Instagram — Primary Platform

### Why It's Primary
This is where the work lives. Outdoor and lifestyle brand creatives and marketers are active here. Portfolio discovery happens here. It's also where the culture of the industry exists — what other studios are doing, what clients are buying, what aesthetic is moving.

### Account Goals
- Build a portfolio feed that functions as a visual resume
- Attract inbound inquiries from outdoor/lifestyle/action sports brands
- Establish Profanity as a studio with a distinct point of view (not just "3D renders")

### Content Mix (rotate through)
1. **Hero shots** — the finished work. Best foot forward. Use for feed posts.
2. **Behind the scenes** — WIP renders, lighting tests, material studies. High engagement, builds process credibility.
3. **Detail shots** — close crops, texture studies, specific moments in a larger project. Gives a project multiple posts.
4. **Studio voice** — opinions on the industry, occasional personal posts from Mark, reactions to things in the space. Use sparingly.

### Posting Cadence
- **Ideal**: 3–4x per week
- **Minimum viable**: 1–2x per week (quality over frequency — one strong post beats four weak ones)
- **Stories**: Can be used for more casual behind-the-scenes, polls, reshares. Lower stakes than feed.
- **Reels**: Prioritize for algorithm reach. Even a simple 15-second render turntable gets pushed more than a static.

### Best Times to Post (EST, adjust for Mark's schedule)
- Tuesday–Thursday: 11am–1pm or 7–9pm
- Saturday: 10am–12pm (outdoor audience is active on weekends)
- Avoid: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons

### Hashtag Strategy
Use 15–20 per post. Structure:

**Tier 1 — Project-specific** (2–3 tags)
Descriptive of the actual product/client/industry: #outdoorgear, #hydrationpack, #runningshoes

**Tier 2 — Niche creative** (5–7 tags)
Where the 3D/CGI community lives: #3drendering, #productvisualization, #cgi, #blender3d, #cinema4d, #octanerender, #houdini

**Tier 3 — Broader reach** (5–8 tags)
Industry and design discovery: #productdesign, #packagingdesign, #brandstyling, #visualdesign, #commercialphotography

**Avoid**: Generic huge tags (#design, #art, #photography) — too much noise, wrong audience.

### Key Accounts to Watch / Engage With
- Other high-quality product viz studios (peer engagement)
- Outdoor brand accounts (potential clients)
- Creative directors at target brands on their personal accounts (not just the brand account)
- Industry publications and newsletters in the outdoor space

## LinkedIn — Secondary Platform

### Why It's Here
Instagram gets the attention. LinkedIn closes the deal. Decision-makers — creative directors, brand managers, marketing VPs at outdoor and lifestyle brands — are on LinkedIn. The work that makes someone save an Instagram post might be the same work that makes someone send a LinkedIn DM asking about availability.

### Account Goals
- Establish Mark as a credible, serious operator in the 3D visualization space
- Make the business case for CGI over traditional photography for outdoor/lifestyle brands
- Stay top of mind with past clients and warm prospects

### Content Mix
1. **Project posts** — same work as Instagram, reframed for business value (what problem it solved, what the client needed)
2. **POV posts** — brief takes on the industry (when CGI makes sense, how the brief-to-delivery process works, what separates good 3D from bad)
3. **Case study fragments** — "Client needed X. Here's how we approached it." Not a full white paper — just a paragraph and an image.

### Posting Cadence
- **Ideal**: 2–3x per week
- **Minimum viable**: 1x per week
- LinkedIn's algorithm rewards consistency more than Instagram's

### Best Times to Post (EST)
- Tuesday–Thursday: 8–10am or 12–2pm
- Avoid weekends — LinkedIn engagement drops sharply

### Hashtag Strategy
Use 3–5 tags maximum. More looks spammy on LinkedIn.
- #3dvisualization or #productvisualization
- #cgi or #3drendering
- One industry-specific tag: #outdoorbrands, #lifestylemarketing, #packagingdesign

### LinkedIn-Specific Tactics
- **First comment**: Post the link to the full project (if applicable) in the first comment, not the caption. LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links in the body.
- **Mention past clients** when appropriate — it shows real-world application.
- **Engage in comments** on posts by target brands and creative directors. Thoughtful comments (not "great work!") build visibility.
- **Connection strategy**: Connect with creative directors and marketing managers at target brands. Don't pitch in the connection request. Just connect.

## Platforms Not Worth Mark's Time Right Now

### TikTok
Potentially interesting longer term for process content. But the editing overhead is high, the audience skews younger than the decision-makers Profanity wants to reach, and the content format doesn't suit Mark's style. **Not worth the investment right now.**

### Twitter / X
The design and CGI community that was there has largely fragmented. Low ROI for the effort. Skip.

### Pinterest
Interesting for passively capturing search traffic on product visualization queries. Low maintenance (auto-post from Instagram). Worth setting up once but not worth active management.

### Behance / ArtStation
Good for portfolio indexing and the CGI/3D community, but these are peer networks, not client networks. Can post finished work there for discovery but don't spend energy engaging.

## Metrics That Matter vs. Metrics That Don't

**Matters:**
- Inbound DMs and inquiries (track these)
- Profile visits from target brands (check who's viewing your LinkedIn)
- Saves on Instagram (indicates real interest, not just scroll-past appreciation)
- Follower quality — are the right people following?

**Doesn't matter (for Profanity's goals):**
- Raw follower count
- Likes on any given post
- Reach/impressions on posts that don't generate saves or inquiries
- Going viral on anything unrelated to the work
