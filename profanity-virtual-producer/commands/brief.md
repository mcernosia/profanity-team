**Identify the document type from the input:**

**Project Brief** — for internal team / specialists kicking off a project
Includes: Project overview, creative direction summary, full deliverable list with specs, timeline with milestones, revision structure, contact / approval chain, asset status (what's provided, what needs to be built), open questions

**Shot List** — for animation or multi-shot projects
Includes: Shot number, description, camera movement, product configuration/variant, duration, special notes, delivery format per shot

**Delivery Spec Sheet** — for handoff to client or agency
Includes: File name conventions, formats, resolutions, frame rates, color space, delivery method (WeTransfer, Dropbox, Frame.io), deadline, point of contact

**Change Order** — when scope changes mid-project
Includes: Original scope reference, description of change requested, impact on timeline, impact on budget (cost to add), whether original deliverables are affected, approval required before proceeding
